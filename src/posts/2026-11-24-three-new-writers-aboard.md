Last month I wrote that the masthead had grown to nine. It has grown again, and this time the new crew found us on their own. This month, three students applied through our Join page, sent in their writing samples, and signed on as staff writers. They are the first members of the paper to come aboard from outside the circle of students who started it, and with Olivia in Barcelona, the paper now files from Europe for the first time.

Please welcome:

**Cade Hamm**, a high school student in South Carolina, joins the Politics desk. He founded *Democracy Under Strain*, a research-based political blog where he interviews elected officials and community members, and he is especially interested in public policy and socioeconomic inequality.

**Anay Gupta**, a sophomore at Goose Creek Memorial High School in Baytown, Texas, and a Lee College dual-credit student, joins the Health desk. He is a competitive debater and National History Day national qualifier, and his first two pieces, [When an Iron Pill Falls Short](/posts/2026-11-24-when-an-iron-pill-falls-short/) and [The Instructions You Read at Home](/posts/2026-11-24-the-instructions-you-read-at-home/), are already up.

**Olivia Grace Ribó Ruffatto** writes from Barcelona, having grown up between Spain and Montana. Her work has appeared in *The Teen Magazine*, and she will cover global affairs, gender, and human rights.

Cade's and Olivia's first bylines will appear here in the coming weeks. Students who would like to join them can find everything they need on our [Join page](/join/).

*Ethan Yang is the second Editor-in-Chief of The Argosy.*
